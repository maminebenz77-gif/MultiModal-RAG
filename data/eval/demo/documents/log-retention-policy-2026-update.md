# Customer Log Retention Policy (Updated)

Effective June 1, 2026, Solstice Systems retains customer-facing
application logs (request logs, access logs, and audit logs) for **90
days** from the time they are written, up from the previous 30-day
window. After 90 days, logs are permanently deleted from all storage
tiers, including backups.

The retention window was extended to 90 days to satisfy the log
availability requirement in our updated SOC 2 Type II audit scope, which
requires customer-facing logs to remain available for at least one full
fiscal quarter. The previous 30-day window was no longer sufficient to
meet that requirement.

Logs older than 90 days cannot be recovered under any circumstances,
including for a customer support request or a legal hold -- if a longer
retention window is ever needed for a specific case, it must be arranged
in advance, before the data ages out.

This policy applies uniformly across all regions and all customer tiers,
and replaces the retention policy that was in effect before June 2026.
