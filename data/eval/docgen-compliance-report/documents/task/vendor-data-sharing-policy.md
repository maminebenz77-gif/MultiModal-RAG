# Vendor and Third-Party Data Sharing Policy

## Before sharing any customer data

Customer data may only be shared with a third-party vendor or
subprocessor if all of the following are in place first:

1. A signed data processing agreement (DPA) covering that vendor,
   reviewed by legal.
2. The vendor is added to the approved subprocessor list maintained by
   the legal team.
3. Only the specific fields the vendor actually needs for the service
   being provided are shared -- full customer records are never handed
   to a vendor by default (data minimization).

## Ongoing vendor obligations

A vendor on the approved subprocessor list must complete an annual
security review with Meridian's security team to remain on the list. A
vendor that fails the review, or that misses the review window, is
removed from the approved list and data sharing with that vendor stops
until the review is redone and passed.

## Customer visibility

Meridian maintains a public subprocessor changelog page listing every
approved vendor and the date it was added or removed. Customers are not
individually notified of routine subprocessor list changes beyond that
page, though material changes are called out in the quarterly customer
newsletter.

## Owner

Maintained jointly by legal and the security engineering team.
